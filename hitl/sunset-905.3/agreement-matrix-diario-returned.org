* Comparison method
Compare explicit recorded values, page inventories, and per-page region counts.
Empty arrays mean no entries recorded, not observed absence. Null ranks and
ambiguity fields mean unknown. Bounding boxes use normalized page coordinates;
viewport pixel sizes are rendering metadata, not conflicting document geometry.

readers segmented pages differently and supplied no labels. Counts alone do
not establish compatible segmentation or a contradiction.
