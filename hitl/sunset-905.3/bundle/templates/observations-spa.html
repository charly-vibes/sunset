Before you start

You are one of two independent human readers. Your job: look at a few documents (carriers) the way a human reads them, and record what you see. Your observations are the reference set — later they are compared with the other reader's, and disagreements are kept, not resolved.

This form works completely offline. Nothing is sent anywhere; your work stays in this browser until you download the files yourself.

What you will do (4 steps)

  1. Tell the form who you are — your reader id (the owner gave it to you: reader-a or reader-b).
  2. Per carrier (4 of them): open the carrier file in a normal viewer next to this window, then fill this form while reading. A stopwatch times you per carrier.
  3. Download one JSON file per carrier — the form does it for you at the end of each carrier.
  4. Zip everything and send it back — a folder named after your reader id, with the 4 JSON files plus a short notes.md. Do not commit to the sunset repo yourself.

Five rules

1. Work separately. Never look at the other reader's sheet or discuss the carriers until both of you have submitted.

2. No extraction tooling. Open the carrier in a normal viewer and read. Do not run pdftotext, PyMuPDF, OCR, or any analysis tooling — your eyes and a normal PDF/EPUB viewer only. The built-in viewer in this form counts as a normal viewer.

3. "unknown" is an answer. Where you cannot determine something, choose unknown — that is a valid, classified result, never a failure.

4. No smoothing. If two readings seem possible (a table that could be two sections, a date that could be issue or publication date), record both as alternatives; do not resolve between readings yourself.

5. Stopwatch: record your minutes per carrier — reviewer effort is part of the measurement.

Ready?

Takes roughly 10–20 minutes per carrier, depending on the document.
