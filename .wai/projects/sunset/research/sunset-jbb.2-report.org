Raw text retains PyMuPDF extraction output, adding a terminal line feed only when needed to delimit a page. Search is the NFC-normalized extracted word sequence joined by one ASCII space. Word maps carry half-open Unicode codepoint offsets independently into raw and search text, the exact raw token, PyMuPDF's unrotated crop-page box, and the box transformed into the declared rotated visible page frame. Line groups remain PyMuPDF's returned groups. Page maps record cropbox, rotation, frame names, and the transformation matrix.

Inspection checks normalization/configuration consistency, raw and search spans, frame mappings, transformed boxes, line spans, and artifact digests. The feature remains native-only and makes no reading-order or semantic claim.

* Limitations

The alignment contract intentionally fails extraction when PyMuPDF's raw text whitespace tokens cannot be aligned one-for-one with its word extractor output. This prevents silent omission, but unusual PDF encodings/layouts may require a future explicit alignment strategy. Focused tests passed; the lead owns ~just spec-check~ and overall verification. No issue closure, commit, push, or pipeline advancement performed.

* Lead smoke follow-up

A lead-side read-only smoke on page 10 of the owner PDF revealed raw text `(Art.9delaLey14967)` whose corresponding word extractor sequence is `(Art.`, `9`, `de`, `la`, `Ley`, `14967)`. Replaced whitespace-token equality with sequential contiguous span alignment. Adjacent word spans can touch while retaining their exact raw substrings; all remaining non-whitespace text must be covered, and irreconcilable sequences still fail explicitly. A helper-level regression generates a PDF from adjacent text fragments with no raw whitespace and passes their known source segments as a simulated extractor word sequence to the alignment helper; it verifies touching spans.
