** Declared conventions (documented in frames.py header — declared, not guessed)
1. Descriptor space = canonical PDF user space (bottom-left, x-right y-up, pt); pixel space = raster top-left y-down at =config.dpi=.
2. =rotation= = clockwise degrees the rendered raster is rotated vs the descriptor page (PDF /Rotate); raster dims swap for 90/270, derived from the caller-supplied raster extent.
3. =crop= non-null = ={"x","y"}= pt added in descriptor space before unit conversion (exactly invertible).
4. =PINNED_PIXEL_TOLERANCE_PX = 1e-6= pins round-trip recovery; rotation expected pixels are hand-computed in tests, never code-re-derived.
