* What it proves

- The 0.3 wire contract is implementable as a strict superset-in-shape of
  the 0.2 top-level fields plus the five mandatory arrays (views, selectors,
  interpretations, coverage, profiles).
- Outcome states are typed: malformed field types yield invalid_shape
  before reference lookup or graph traversal; malformed JSON yields
  parse_error; the frozen 0.2 validator rejects 0.3 descriptors with its
  0.2 vocabulary and is never upgraded in place.
- Pinned profile identity works: digest identity beats the URI hint; a
  registry change under a pin is detected (profile_digest_mismatch).
- Evidence binding is replayable: content_ids + selector_ids resolve, and
  the anchor reproduces the observation on the exact (unnormalized) value.

* What it deliberately leaves out

- PDF/EPUB adapters and real-carrier fixtures (sunset-r68 tasks 3.1).
- Coverage inventories and scoped dispositions (tasks 2.2, 3.2, 3.3).
- Cross-reader benchmark and adversarial conformance corpus (tasks 5.1-5.4,
  the first-consumer demonstration milestone).
- Migration from 0.2 descriptors (tasks 4.1, 4.2).
- Ontology boundary projections (task 6.1, after the demonstration).
- Transform/lineage replay beyond the single source link (sunset-r68.2 and
  the evidence lineage checks in tasks 1.3).
